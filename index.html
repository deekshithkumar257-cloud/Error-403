Apollo 11 LM Descent Stage
NSSDCA ID: 1969-059C 		Landed July 20, 1969 at 20:17:40 UTC in Mare Tranquillitatis (0.67408° N, 23.47297° E). Served as stationary launch pad for Ascent Stage. Descent stage remained on the Moon; Ascent stage crashed into the Moon after rendezvous. LRRR retroreflector array requires zero power and is still active today. NASA Apollo 11 Mission ↗
Apollo Lunar Roving Vehicles
NSSDCA: 1971-063D 		Three rovers used during Apollo 15, 16, and 17 (1971–1972). Traversed 90.3 km total; collected 280+ kg of samples including the 4.1-billion-year-old Genesis Rock. Apollo 11 had NO rover. Exactly three rovers were driven on the Moon, and all three remain parked on the lunar surface. ALSJ LRV Operations ↗
Viking 1 Lander
NSSDCA ID: 1975-075C 		First successful U.S. Mars lander, touching down July 20, 1976 on Chryse Planitia (22.48° N, 47.97° W). Operated for 2,245 sols until Nov 1982. Viking 1 DID NOT discover life on Mars. Soil gas release was caused by inorganic chemical oxidants (perchlorates), with zero organic molecules detected by GCMS. NASA JPL Viking 1 ↗
Sojourner Rover
NSSDCA ID: 1996-068B 		First mobile rover driven on Mars (July 5, 1997 in Ares Vallis). Mass 11.5 kg; Rocker-Bogie suspension; APXS discovered andesite volcanic rocks. Planned for only 7 Martian sols; operated for 83 Martian sols (85 Earth days), nearly 12 times its design requirement. JPL Sojourner Archive ↗
Mars Pathfinder Lander
NSSDCA ID: 1996-068A 		Landed July 4, 1997 via 24 Kevlar airbags. Delivered Sojourner. IMP camera proved catastrophic ancient mega-floods in Ares Vallis. Pathfinder and Sojourner are two distinct pieces of hardware. Renamed the 'Carl Sagan Memorial Station' upon landing. NASA Science Pathfinder ↗
Voyager 1 Spacecraft
NSSDCA ID: 1977-084A 		Launched Sept 5, 1977. Crossed Heliopause into interstellar space on Aug 25, 2012 (121.6 AU). Currently > 163 AU (> 24.4 billion km) from Earth. NOT an abandoned object on a planet! It is STILL TRAVELLING at ~17 km/s through interstellar space; all 4 working science instruments were restored in 2024. Voyager Interstellar Mission ↗
